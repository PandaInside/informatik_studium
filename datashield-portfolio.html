Executive Summary

Who We Are

DataShield is a dual-focus cybersecurity firm built by IT students with a clear mission: protect individuals from hidden data surveillance, and equip businesses with vulnerability intelligence before attackers can exploit it.

Most people have no idea how much data they leak every single day — to advertisers, data brokers, and malicious trackers. Meanwhile, companies assume their systems are secure — until they aren't.

DataShield bridges both gaps: our ShieldBlock platform gives consumers control over their digital footprint, while our PenProbe service delivers deep penetration testing for businesses that can't afford to find out the hard way. One firm. Two shields.

Case Studies

Results We've Delivered

Three real projects, analyzed through the STAR method to show how we think, execute, and measure success.

E-Commerce Data Leak Audit
SME Online Retailer · PenProbe Engagement
01
Situation

A mid-size online retailer suspected customer data was being exposed via their checkout flow but had no visibility into their actual vulnerability surface.

Task

Identify all data exposure points in the web application, targeting payment and PII fields, within a 2-week engagement window.

Action

Ran OWASP Top 10 testing, intercepted API traffic with Burp Suite, and performed SQL injection probes on checkout and user-profile endpoints.

Result

Found 3 critical vulnerabilities including an exposed API key. Client patched all issues within 1 week — zero data breach since.

ShieldBlock Browser Extension MVP
Consumer Product · Internal Development
02
Situation

Research showed 78% of popular websites embed 10+ hidden trackers. Most ad-blockers miss fingerprinting scripts entirely.

Task

Build a Chromium-compatible extension that blocks trackers in real time while maintaining a lightweight footprint under 5 MB.

Action

Developed in JavaScript using Chrome's declarativeNetRequest API. Integrated EasyPrivacy filter lists and built a custom fingerprint randomizer.

Result

Blocked an average of 24 trackers per session. Page load time improved by 18% on ad-heavy news sites.

Internal Network Audit — Law Firm
Professional Services · PenProbe Engagement
03
Situation

A local law firm handling sensitive client documents had never had a formal security audit. Staff used shared passwords and outdated software.

Task

Full internal network assessment with a prioritized remediation roadmap aligned with GDPR data handling requirements.

Action

Used Nmap for network mapping, Nessus for vulnerability scanning, and ran a simulated phishing campaign against 15 staff members.

Result

60% clicked the phishing link initially → 0% after training. GDPR compliance gap closed in 3 weeks.
